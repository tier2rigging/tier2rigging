Slings, Webbing & Rigging Hardware

Tier2 Rigging LLC supplies lifting slings, webbing, cargo tie-down and rigging hardware to DLA and other government and industrial buyers. We source exact approved parts from established manufacturers, then package and mark them to military standards in-house in Manorville, New York.

Request a quote

Core capabilities

  • Exact-product supply of approved-source parts, with manufacturer traceability on every order
  • Military packaging and marking in-house to MIL-STD-2073 and MIL-STD-129, including bar-coded shipping labels
  • Rigging and textile expertise from an FAA Master Parachute Rigger with an in-house industrial sewing shop
  • Fast, responsive quoting through DIBBS and direct request

What we supply

  • Lifting slings polyester web slings with eye or triangle hardware
  • Webbing and cordage nylon and polyester webbing, rope, cord and line
  • Cargo tie-down straps and tie-down assemblies
  • Rigging hardware commercial hardware, fittings and parachute components
